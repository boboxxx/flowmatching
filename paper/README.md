# FlowHARQ VTC 2027 technical draft

This directory is an evidence-linked technical draft, not a paper that should
be submitted without author review.  The VTC 2027-Spring policy states that AI
tools may edit author-written text but may not replace an author in generating
article content.  Before submission, the authors must rewrite/review the prose,
verify every claim and citation, and add the required AI-use disclosure.

Numerical claims are injected from `generated/results_macros.tex`, which is
generated from held-out CSV files.  Do not replace those macros with manually
typed numbers.

Target: IEEE VTC 2027-Spring, five pages without overlength charges (up to seven
pages with charges).  Official deadline checked on 19 September 2026: 30
September 2026.
