# Research decision after the completed feasibility stage

2026-09-20. This decision reports measurements, not a successful new HARQ method.

The first frozen-Swin-decoder construction is stopped. A completed at 900 updates;
B failed numerically after its last logged update 1950; the bounded B-fixed completed
at 3200 updates under its declared early-stopping rule. Both completed candidates
have zero selective saving under the registered oracle gate. All nine registered
service-threshold combinations also return zero saving. B-fixed's all-group MSE
is 0.002393736, versus 0.002362082 for the base; PSNR is 28.507 versus 28.554 dB.
Thus useful progressive refinement has not been established before any generative
model is added. No conditional-posterior or request-policy training was launched.

The four-image fixed-noise diagnostic demonstrates that the trainable enhancement
path can fit training examples; it does not explain the failure to generalize.
Frozen decoder capacity, the latent-correction parameterization and optimization
remain hypotheses, not isolated causal findings. These runs do not disprove all
progressive codecs or all posterior-guided evidence-acquisition systems.

## What is actually complete

- Paid packet normalization, unit complex-symbol power, explicit feedback accounting,
  source-disjoint split hashes and one training seed (2027).
- Two full all-subsets development evaluations: 5,120 reconstruction metric rows.
- Three published author-model families under a common paid-metadata protocol:
  SwinJSCC, NTSCC and CDDM; 1,680 per-image/channel/method rows on Kodak24.
  CDDM's two direct-JSCC controls are not two extra published methods.
- Mathematical definitions for causal decisions, conditional flow training, prospective
  evidence value and finite-sample ACK risk; their implementation/training status is
  explicitly distinguished from the completed codec tests.

## What is not complete

This is not a publication-ready VTC project. There is no measured posterior-policy
gain, no operational learned ACK policy, no 5% ACK-risk certificate, no fading/vehicle
channel result, no broad SNR frontier and no faithful numerical SAFG-HARQ reproduction.
The direct selective-HARQ comparator remains necessary despite completed non-feedback
and generative-receiver baselines. DeepJSCC-l is verified literature, not a numerical
result in this package. Existing DeepJSCC-f ideal-output-feedback results retain their
separate protocol and cannot be relabelled as finite-feedback results.

## Next defensible study, not an automatic continuation of this candidate

First establish an actually useful progressive representation, potentially with a
jointly trained enhancement decoder rather than correction into the frozen base
decoder's latent space. Keep the original base-only path as a control; compare
joint-versus-frozen decoding at the same paid budget and report whether each extra
group improves held-out distortion/perceptual quality. This needs a declared new
codec study, not another loss/seed sweep to make this failed gate pass.

Only a codec that refines successfully merits a new oracle gate and then posterior
uncertainty tests. The observed 40-image gate is now development data; new confirmatory
claims need fresh source-disjoint evaluation. Kodak has been evaluated for published
baselines only, not for candidate selection. The 40-image calibration pilot cannot
certify 5% conditional false-ACK risk at 95% confidence, even with zero observed errors.
Do not train a posterior to compensate for an absent evidence benefit, relax the
10% criterion retrospectively, or write superiority claims around these negative runs.
